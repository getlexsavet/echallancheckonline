What Is an eChallan in Pakistan?

An eChallan is an electronic or digital traffic challan record associated with a reported traffic violation. Depending on the traffic authority and the system used, a challan may be generated when a vehicle is identified in connection with a traffic rule violation.

The available record may include important information such as:

  • Vehicle registration number
  • Challan or reference number
  • Date and time of the violation
  • Location details
  • Type of traffic violation
  • Traffic fine amount
  • Current challan status

The exact information available may differ depending on the province, city, and relevant traffic authority.

How to Stay Safe From Fake eChallan Messages

Drivers should be careful when receiving traffic-related SMS messages, emails, or links. A message claiming that you have received an eChallan should not automatically be trusted.

Before opening a link or entering any information:

  • Check the sender carefully
  • Avoid sharing OTPs or passwords
  • Do not provide unnecessary banking information
  • Verify the challan through an official or trusted source
  • Check the vehicle and challan details independently
  • Be cautious of urgent or suspicious payment requests

A legitimate traffic record should be verified through the appropriate authority before any payment is made.
